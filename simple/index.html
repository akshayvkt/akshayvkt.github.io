About

Curiosity drives me. AI and clean energy are two things that live rent-free in my head, and my life's goal is to make lasting contributions towards a future that's both exciting and sustainable.

Currently, I'm a founder, building a product with the objective of helping people live better lives.

Previously, I've worked in clean energy, building software products for installing and maintaining residential solar and storage systems at Generac, SunPower, and Tesla.

Before that, I got a Master's in chemical engineering from Cornell and a bachelor's in chemical engineering from Osmania University in India.

Projects

Over the last two years, I kept returning, often without realizing it, to one question:

If intelligence becomes cheap, abundant and capable of acting, what should its relationship with an individual human actually look like?

These are some of the products I designed and built with AI while exploring that question.
