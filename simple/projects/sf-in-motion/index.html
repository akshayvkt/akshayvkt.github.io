SF in Motion

See how SF moves on Lyft Bike

Built
September 2026

I'm a daily user of Lyft Bike. When I open the app to find bikes, I see a station have 15 bikes at one time of the day. At a different time, there's just 2. On a weekend at that same time, there could be 8.

That made me wonder - how does the fleet of Lyft Bikes move around SF? What story does the data tell?

SF in Motion was a result of that.
