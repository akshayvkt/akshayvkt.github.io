Tidy

began as Image Namer

Never let your Desktop screen fill up with a bunch of screenshots

Built
December 2025

Why I made it

Screenshots and downloaded images quietly accumulate meaningless filenames. I wanted to explore AI that improves the environment behind the scenes rather than waiting in a chat box for instructions.

What it became

The first utility watched images and used Gemini's visual understanding to give them useful names. It later became a small API product with image summaries, signup, usage tracking, file validation and rate limits.

What happened

Tidy stayed a compact experiment, but it made a distinct interface idea tangible: some of the most useful AI may be software we rarely address directly because it continuously takes care of small cognitive chores.
