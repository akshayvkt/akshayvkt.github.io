Lenny Podcast MCP

Let your AI guide you with Lenny's podcast wisdom

Built
January 2026
Link
View source ↗

Lenny's Podcast has hundreds of useful conversations, but it was hard to remember which guest talked about a problem, or to find the original context.

So I built an MCP over all the podcast transcripts. You add it to Claude or ChatGPT, ask a question, and it searches the transcripts, pulls the episode and answers with quotes from what the guest said.

Claire Vo's ChatPRD lists it in their Lenny's Podcast transcripts repo.
