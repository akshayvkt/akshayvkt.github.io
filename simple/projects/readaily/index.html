Readaily

One book-grounded essay each day, chosen for what matters to you.

Built
May–July 2026

There’s 1000s of books that I’ll never get to read.

If I live to 80, and I read 2 books a month, I can still only read a little over 1000 books.

Many books I won’t get to know and enjoy, but there’s many books I’d want to learn from - many books I wouldn’t know I needed.

That’s why I built Readaily - it does one simple thing well.

Every morning at 7am, it sends me an essay in the style of writing I enjoy - one that takes me no longer than 5 minutes.

That’s it. There’s no 1000 generic book summaries, no content library lying around. Just one thing.

After I read it, I pick one book for tomorrow - it offers me 5 suggestions everyday based on what it thinks I like - so I either pick a new book or continue with the same one.

Next day, I have another 5 minute essay to read. I’ve been enjoying this - looking forward everyday to the essay that lands tomorrow.
