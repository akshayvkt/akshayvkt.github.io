      Sound

      Everything you hear runs on San Francisco's clock. When fog comes in, the four foghorns of the Golden Gate sound their real Coast Guard patterns, each reaching you late by the seconds it takes sound to cross the water. Ships give their fog signal, the Hyde Street cable car rings on its timetable, and everyone on the site hears the same horn at the same second.

      Nothing recorded, everything made.

      None of the sounds in the scene were recorded.
